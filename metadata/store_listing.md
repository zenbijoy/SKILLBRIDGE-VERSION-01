## Full Description
Welcome to SkillBridge, the ultimate campus network designed to connect you with peers who want to learn what you know—and teach what you don't. 

**Features:**
- **Realtime Video Rooms:** Join spontaneous study sessions with high-quality WebRTC video and audio.
- **Smart Matching:** Our algorithm recommends peers, research groups, and mentors based on your university context and skill goals.
- **Campus Events & Clubs:** Stay updated on local campus happenings and join verified campus organizations.
- **Gamified Reputation:** Build your teaching reputation through peer reviews, leaderboard standings, and earned badges.
- **Secure Campus Chat:** Message peers securely without exposing personal contact information.

Join thousands of students turning their campuses into active, collaborative learning hubs. Master a new skill today, or share your expertise to help others!
