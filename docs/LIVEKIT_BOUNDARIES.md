# LiveKit Scope & System Boundaries

## 1. When LiveKit IS Used
- **Scheduled Peer Learning Rooms**: 3 to 16 students in interactive classroom.
- **CT Preparation Sessions**: 3 to 6 students with synchronized syllabus, quick quizzes, and audio/video doubt clearing.
- **Post-Broadcast Interactive Q&A**: Selected 3–5 attendees invited on stage after YouTube Live broadcasts.

## 2. When LiveKit is NOT Used
- **1-to-1 Audio & Video Calls**: Handled 100% via Raw WebRTC P2P to conserve LiveKit participant minutes.
- **Large Club Broadcasts (100–1000+ Viewers)**: Handled via YouTube Live Embed with SkillBridge metadata.
