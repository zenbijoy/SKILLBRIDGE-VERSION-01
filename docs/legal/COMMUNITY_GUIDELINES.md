# SkillBridge Community Guidelines

SkillBridge is a collaborative learning community designed to empower peer-to-peer education, mentorship, and skill development in a respectful, supportive environment.

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Mutual Respect & Inclusivity
- Treat all learners, tutors, and moderators with dignity regardless of background, identity, or skill level.
- Constructive feedback and healthy technical debate are encouraged; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or hate speech are strictly prohibited.

### 2. Academic & Collaboration Integrity
- Share knowledge to help others understand concepts, not to complete assignments dishonestly.
- Do not plagiarize or distribute copyrighted learning materials without permission.

### 3. Safe Communication
- Do not share sensitive personal information (such as government IDs, credit card numbers, home addresses, or private credentials) in public rooms or group chats.
- Report suspicious behavior, spam, unsolicited commercial advertisements, or abusive users immediately via the in-app Report button.

---

## Enforcement & Sanctions
Violations are investigated by human moderators and logged to immutable audit trails:
- **First Infraction**: Educational warning or temporary room mute.
- **Moderate Infraction**: Content removal and temporary account suspension (24 to 72 hours).
- **Severe / Repeated Infraction**: Permanent ban and device token revocation.
